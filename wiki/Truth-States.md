# Truth States

Back to [[Home]].

Truth states:

1. `claimed`
2. `observed`
3. `verified`
4. `committed`
5. `pushed`
6. `deployed`
7. `delivered`

## Rules

- `claimed` is not `verified`.
- `observed` is not `verified`.
- `committed` is not `pushed`.
- `pushed` is not `deployed`.
- Benchmark results require actual benchmark evidence.
